Basic Sandwich Recipe

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • tomato
  • cucumber
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Onions
  • oil/ghee/butter (optional)

Cooking Steps

  1. Cut and slice all the vegetables
  2. Take a bread and cover inner layer of both sides with Tomato Sauce
  3. Add the vegetable Slices
    4. (Below steps are optional)
  4. On a pan add some butter
  5. Cook the Sandwich until Goldern Brown GBD
  6. Cook the other side as well

Enjoy your Sandwich
